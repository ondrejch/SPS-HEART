Kaon s-dependence in p+p interactions

The total yields and pT integrated invariant cross sections F(xF=0) of K+, KS0 and K- as a function of √s from 2.8 to 630 GeV.

Total yields: In the figure is presented the total yields of K+, KS0 and K- as a function of √s. The points represent the measurements from different experiments and the full lines through the results are eyeball fits.

Invariant cross sections F(xF=0): In the figure is presented F(xF=0) of K+, KS0 and K- as a function of √s. The points represent the measurements from different experiments and the full lines through the results are eyeball fits.
